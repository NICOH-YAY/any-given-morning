A game about meaning

Any Given Morning

A small farm, a small day. You will not be here long. Wander, talk, help someone, or do nothing at all. Then a new morning starts somewhere else.

Keys: WASD or arrows move · E, Space or Enter interact · Esc menu. On a phone, use the on-screen pad.

The Mortality switch on the menu is the one rule you can change. Play a round with it on, then a round with it off, and notice what you did.
