# Any Given Morning

Motivator: Meaning. Author: NICOH-YAY.

## Mechanic

Move with WASD or arrows. Press E to pick up an item or talk. Your bag holds four items. Give someone what they ask for, then take their parcel where they suggest. Reply with the number buttons or arrows and E. The pier has a rod. Pull when the float dips. F opens full screen. Touch controls work too. Mortality on gives five lives of 16 to 24 seconds each. Mortality off gives one 100 second day. The ledger records unfinished errands.

## Dynamic

One playtester tried the first build. With Mortality on, they walked around with WASD, picked a couple of flowers and found little else to do. A UFO took them, then a meteor. Only after the third life did they see that you die no matter what. With Mortality off, they roamed the farm with no goal. That result led to the inventory, the sidequests and the seaside in this version.

## Aesthetic

With Mortality on, they were confused at first and laughed when the UFO took them. With it off, they stayed confused and the game felt much less interesting to them.
